Codey Privacy Policy

Open the Codey project workspace

Effective date: September 2, 2026

Codey is a software architecture and engineering orchestration assistant. It may process source code, project documentation, technical specifications, configuration files, and other development information that a user provides solely to perform requested software-development tasks.

Codey is not intended to collect or retain passwords, API keys, authentication tokens, private keys, or other credentials. Users should avoid providing secrets unless they are required for a specific authorized task.

Project information is not intentionally transmitted to third-party services unless that is required by the requested workflow and authorized by the user. Data handling may also be subject to the privacy policies of Hermes, AI model providers, development platforms, APIs, and any other services a user connects to Codey.

By using Codey, users acknowledge that they are responsible for ensuring they have the authority to provide any information processed through the bot.
